Shondhaan
Find trusted professionals. Get things done.

Case study

The challenge

Finding a reliable electrician, plumber, AC technician or cleaner often means asking friends, searching Facebook groups or calling multiple people before finding someone available. Information is scattered, service quality can vary and finding the right professional takes time.

Our solution

Shondhaan brings customers and service providers together through a single digital marketplace. Customers can discover services, explore provider information, submit service requests and connect with professionals — making the process of finding and arranging services simpler and more convenient.
